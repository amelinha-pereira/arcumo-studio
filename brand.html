Primary Text

Arc Amber

The only warm element. Use for accents, highlights, the logo icon, and single emphasis words. Never use as a background. Never use for large text blocks.

Obsidian & Void

The default canvas. Obsidian for elevated surfaces, Void for the base layer. The difference is subtle — it creates depth without distraction.

Ash & Bone

Ash is for secondary text, labels, metadata. Bone is for primary content text and headlines. Never pure white — it's too harsh against the dark backgrounds.

04 — Voice

Brand Voice

Precise

No fluff. Every word earns its place. Say the thing once, say it well, stop.

Curious

You explore, experiment, and share the process. The work is always in motion.

Confident

Not arrogant. You know what you're building even when it's new territory.

Sounds like

  • A director's commentary — insider, deliberate
  • Short sentences. Room to breathe.
  • Someone who's done the thing, not just read about it
  • Process as part of the work
  • Concrete, specific, no buzzwords

Never sounds like

  • Corporate — "synergy", "leverage", "solutions"
  • Hype — "🔥 INSANE results!!"
  • Apologetic — "just a small studio"
  • Vague — "innovative creative experiences"
  • Rushed — walls of text, no white space
